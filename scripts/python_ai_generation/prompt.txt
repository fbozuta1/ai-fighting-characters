Create a PNG reference sprite for a 2D pixel art fighting game character designed to be easy to animate and easy to estimate as a skeleton.

STYLE & CAMERA
- High-detail pixel art, modern retro style, 16-bit inspired but detailed
- Orthographic camera
- Strict side-view silhouette, facing right/east
- Full body visible, centered in frame
- Neutral animation reference pose, not a final action pose

SKELETON ESTIMATION REQUIREMENTS
- Arms and legs clearly visible and slightly separated from the torso
- Elbows, hands, knees, feet, shoulders, hips, neck, and head readable in the silhouette
- No crossed limbs
- No cropped body parts
- No extreme crouch, jump, lunge, kick, punch, or foreshortened pose
- Weapon or gear may be present, but must not hide the torso, arms, hips, knees, or feet

RESOLUTION & SCALE
- Working resolution: 1024x1024 pixels
- Intended to be downscaled to 64x64, 96x96, or 256x256
- Character occupies about 70-85% of the canvas height
- Small pixel size, high pixel density

PIXEL ART CONSTRAINTS
- Pixel-perfect rendering
- No blur
- No smooth gradients
- No anti-aliasing
- Clearly defined pixel clusters
- Crisp edges

COLOR & LIGHTING
- High contrast lighting
- Bright, saturated mid-tones
- Deep shadows and strong highlights
- Subtle hue shifting, with warm highlights and cool shadows
- Light source from top-left

POSE & PRESENTATION
- Balanced, grounded neutral stance
- Combat-ready but readable, like a rigging/reference pose
- Character identity, outfit, weapon, and gear clearly readable

BACKGROUND
- Transparent background
- No text
- No UI
- No borders
